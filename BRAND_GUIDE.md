# UniHunt — Brand Foundation (v1)

A starting brand system for the study-abroad and overseas-careers venture.
This is v1, meant to grow into campaign templates, social kits, leaflets and brochures.

## 1. The name
**UniHunt** — the search for the right university, course, licence and career, abroad.
Always written as one word, "UniHunt" (capital U, capital H). Never "Uni Hunt" or "Unihunt".

## 2. Positioning
UniHunt guides two audiences, end to end:
- **Students** who want to study abroad (Study Abroad track).
- **Professionals**, mainly healthcare, who want to work abroad (Careers Abroad track).
Focus: Dubai first, with selected UK and Germany programmes.
It currently runs as a standalone brand.

## 3. Taglines (pick one to standardise)
1. **Find your future abroad.**  ← currently live on the site
2. Your path abroad, guided.
3. Study. Work. Belong. Abroad.

## 4. Logo
- Mark: a destination **pin** containing a **north-star** — "find your way, aim high."
- Files: `public/brand/logo.svg` (colour), `public/brand/logo-white.svg` (dark backgrounds),
  `public/brand/icon.svg` (mark only), `public/favicon.svg`.
- Clear space: keep at least the height of the pin around the logo.
- Do not: stretch, recolour the wordmark arbitrarily, or place the colour logo on busy/dark backgrounds (use the white version).

## 5. Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand / Violet 700 | `#6D28D9` | Primary brand, headings accents |
| Interactive / Violet 600 | `#7C3AED` | Links, focus, hovers |
| Accent / Emerald | `#059669` | Primary CTA buttons, "go/success" |
| Spark / Amber | `#F59E0B` | Sparingly, for highlights and badges |
| Ink | `#0F172A` | Headlines, dark sections |
| Body | `#334155` | Body text |
| Canvas | `#FBF9FF` | Soft section backgrounds |
| Line | `#EAE4F7` | Borders, dividers |

Contrast: body and CTA colours meet WCAG AA on white. Keep it that way.

## 6. Typography
- **Display: Outfit** (700/600) — headlines, section titles.
- **Body: Inter** (400/500) — paragraphs and UI.
- Base 16px, line-height 1.5. Headings use tight tracking (-0.02em).

## 7. Voice
Warm, clear, practical and honest. Indian business tone. Speak to aspiration
("find your future") but never over-promise. Avoid jargon. No fabricated stats or claims.

## 8. Imagery
Prefer real, authentic photography of students, professionals and destinations once available.
Avoid obviously AI-generated people. Abstract gradient/mesh textures in the brand palette are fine.

## 9. Next in the brand system (future phases)
- Social post templates (LinkedIn, Instagram) using this palette and Outfit/Inter.
- Lead-gen ad creative templates.
- Leaflet and brochure layouts.
- Real photography and (with permission) partner/university logos.
